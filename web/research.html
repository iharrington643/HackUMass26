// 01 · derivations

The math we used

oxygen & drugs

Steady-state reaction-diffusion

Diffusion in, first-order consumption by living cells (u = 1 where a cell is alive). Oxygen changes in seconds and cells in hours, so we solve for the steady state each step with red-black SOR (ω = 1.7).

0 = D∇²C − k·u·C  ⟹  L = √(D/k)

In a sphere of radius R with C = 1 at the edge: C(r) = R·sinh(r/L) / (r·sinh(R/L)). Oxygen: L ≈ 3 cells (~60 µm). SOURCED

Rayne's derivation

One capillary (Krogh cylinder)

Drug leaving a single capillary into the tissue around it, in cylindrical coordinates:

∂C/∂t = D_eff · (1/r)·∂/∂r(r·∂C/∂r) − k·C

Same length scale, converted to cells: L = √(D_eff/k) / 20 µm. We checked the quasi-steady assumption for each drug (carboplatin is the one that spreads fast enough to matter). RAYNE

drug killing

Hill dose-response with resistance

kill = k_max · Cⁿ / (Cⁿ + (EC50·F)ⁿ)

F = resistance fold for resistant cells (from GDSC: exp(q90 − median of ln IC50)); drug pumps divide C by 6; resting cells get a lower k_max. Rates convert to per-step chances with p = 1 − e−rate·dt. PROXY

radiation

Linear-quadratic + oxygen effect

S = exp(−α·f·d − β·(f·d)²),   f = (K + 3·O₂) / (3·(K + O₂))

α = 0.3 /Gy, β = 0.03 /Gy², maximum oxygen enhancement ratio 3, K = 3 mmHg (Alper & Howard-Flanders form). f = 1 with plenty of oxygen, 1/3 with none. Lethally hit cells are "doomed" and die over ~30 h. SOURCED

persisters & immunity

Switching and activation

Cells under drug switch to a tolerant state at 0.024/day and back after ~96 h. T cells diffuse from the edge (L = 5 cells); pembrolizumab has a 26-day half-life and boosts activation; visibility grows with mutation count. ESTIMATE RAYNE

surrogate

Conformalized quantile regression

Fit quantile models q̂lo, q̂hi on log takeover day, then on a separate calibration set compute scores and widen every interval by their quantile:

E = max(q̂_lo − y, y − q̂_hi)  →  [q̂_lo − Q, q̂_hi + Q]

This guarantees coverage on new data from the same distribution (Romano et al., 2019).

// 03 · GDSC

Drug potency from ~1,000 cell lines

From the GDSC2 screen we took each cancer's median IC50 relative to a typical one of our 10 cancers (softened by a square root, shrunk toward 1 with few cell lines) as a potency multiplier. Close relatives stand in for drugs GDSC didn't test.

Potency multiplier per cancer

1.0 = typical. Higher = the drug works better on that cancer's cell lines.

Resistance fold

How much more drug the most resistant 10% of cell lines need vs the median.
