// pipeline

How it works

Each of our five steps uses data derived from experimentation or calculation in health literature where we could find it, and every number we calculated ourselves as amateurs is labeled in the code.

Grow

36,000-site lattice, 20 µm per cell, 4 hours per step, cells divide into free space.

Feed

Oxygen and each drug soak in from the blood supply (we use the reaction-diffusion model). The core starves of oxygen and dies out, but drugs also can't penetrate that far.

Mutate

8 traits, from faster growth to drug pumps to hiding from T cells

Treat

Chemo, hormones, an antibody-drug conjugate, immunotherapy, light therapy and radiation

Forecast

After 3 weeks of treatment, a model trained on 1200 runs predicts if and when resistance will eventually take over.

What you can do with it

01

Live 3D tumor

Orbit, slice and recolor 36,000 cells by resistance, oxygen, drug level or clone family.

02

Evolution on screen

Treatment kills sensitive cells and resistant clones take over in their place.

03

Treatment regimens

15 schedules from carboplatin every 21 days to 60 Gy radiation, with doses that rise and fall by half-life.

04

ML forecast

Gradient-boosted trees plus conformal calibration: a point of no return day apprximation with a range that is accurate 80% of the time.

05

LED tumor wall Recreation

Five 16×16 LED panels show live slices of the sphere in memory of a physical model we built that we had to abandon due to a faulty adapter.

06

Pill-bottle console

Put an RFID-tagged bottle on the reader, turn the dose and time dials, press go. No mouse needed.

From physics to prediction

Every simulated patient is different: mutation rate, growth speed, drug, radiation and immune strength are drawn at random. We ran 1200 of them through 15 regimens and 10 cancers, then trained a model on only what a doctor could see: the tumor's size during the first three weeks.

The result is off by 13.4 days on runs it never saw, against 22.9 days for the best guess without ML. Its ranges are calibrated, so an "80% range" contains the true day 78% of the time.
